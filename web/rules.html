Entry

Entry to Neon Backgammon Tournament is completely free. No purchase, payment, or virtual-currency stake of any kind is required or accepted to register, join a tournament room, or compete for a prize. Watching an advertisement is never a condition of entry.

Eligibility

Players must be 18 years of age or older, attested at signup. This demo build does not yet enforce a geo-eligibility allowlist/blocklist; production launch will restrict entry to approved regions per SPEC §2.7, expanding only after legal review.

How winners are determined

Each tournament is single-elimination. Rosters are randomly seeded with a cryptographically secure shuffle. Players are paired match by match; the loser of each match is eliminated. The last remaining player is the tournament champion. Backgammon moves, dice rolls, and match outcomes are determined entirely server-side — the game engine is deterministic and auditable.

Prize calculation

The tournament's prize pool equals a configured payout rate (currently 50%) of the estimated advertising revenue attributed to that tournament. In this demo build, ad revenue is simulated (a fixed stub value per impression) rather than sourced from a live ad network, so all prize amounts are virtual credit, not real currency. In a production deployment, prize amounts are estimates until a monthly reconciliation against actual network-reported revenue; a tournament with zero attributed ad revenue pays a $0.00 prize.

Payout terms

This demo build credits winnings to an in-app ledger balance only — no withdrawal mechanism exists yet. A production deployment will require reaching a minimum payout threshold and completing identity verification before funds can be withdrawn via a third-party payout provider.
